OUR STORY

A business born from friendship

Started by childhood friends right in the middle of the pandemic, SERWIS turned a former industrial shop into something genuinely unique: a place where specialty coffee, Neapolitan pizza, and craft beer from local breweries share space with a full bicycle service shop. Coffee cuppings, group rides through post-industrial Silesia, and traveler meetups — a real community built one ride and one cup at a time.
